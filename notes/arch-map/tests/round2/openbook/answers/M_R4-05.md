CANDIDATE 1 (most likely)
FILE: src/backend/commands/subscriptioncmds.c
FUNCTION: AlterSubscription (ALTER_SUBSCRIPTION_OPTIONS, two_phase branch)
DEFECT: The "prepared transactions exist" check is only done when sub->twophasestate == ENABLED. The state can be PENDING while the publisher slot is already two_phase=true and prepared GIDs exist on the subscriber: (a) ENABLED then SET (two_phase=true) is rewritten to PENDING unconditionally, then SET (two_phase=false) skips LookupGXactBySubid; (b) apply worker started streaming with twophase=true but crashed before UpdateTwoPhaseState(ENABLED). ALTER_REPLICATION_SLOT then disables the slot's two_phase while prepared xacts are pending.
FAILURE: Subscription with open prepared transactions has two_phase turned off without the error. The later COMMIT PREPARED on the publisher is decoded as a normal commit and the whole txn is replayed again: duplicate rows or conflicts.
RULE: Disabling two_phase (slot and subscription) is allowed only if no prepared transactions of the subscription exist, whatever the tri-state value.
CONFIDENCE: medium

FUNCTION: AlterSubscription (two_phase branch, worker check)
DEFECT: logicalrep_workers_find(subid, true, true) uses only_running=true, which skips workers that are in_use but have no proc yet (just launched). RULE: No apply/tablesync/parallel worker may be alive or startable during the two_phase change of the slot and catalog.
CONFIDENCE: low

FUNCTION: AlterSubscription (two_phase branch)
