A GIN fix made pending-list cleanup safe against concurrent cleaners: previously a cleanup that saw another process cleaning could exit early, and VACUUM could then miss tuples that it needed to delete. The fix serialized cleanup of the pending list with a lock and made VACUUM wait for it, and also let automatic maintenance stop at the tail of the list as of the start of cleanup rather than chase concurrent inserts. The cleanup is required because tuples recently inserted into the pending list can contain row pointers that VACUUM already considers dead.
