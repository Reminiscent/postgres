## R1-04
FILES: src/backend/commands/async.c
ANSWER: The SRF pg_listening_channels walks the shared channel hash with a dynahash seq scan kept open across calls (hash_seq_search over multiple SRF calls) while holding a lock (likely shared lock on the channel table / lock released between calls). Problems: the lock cannot be held across calls (and is released), so concurrent LISTEN/UNLISTEN modifies the table during the scan, giving skipped/duplicate/incorrect results or scan corruption; also an open seq scan is not cleaned up on error/early termination. Violates scan consistency; correct fix is to collect the channel names in one call (value-per-call from a local list/ array under the lock, or materialize).
CONFIDENCE: medium for (a); medium for (b)
DRILL: Read pg_listening_channels in async.c and the channel hash (GlobalChannelTable) locking.

## R1-07
FILES: src/backend/storage/lmgr/lock.c, src/backend/storage/lmgr/deadlock.c, src/backend/storage/lmgr/proc.c, src/backend/access/transam/parallel.c
ANSWER: Original made relation-extension lock conflict among lock-group members but the deadlock detector / lock-group handling did not account for it: a leader holding the lock (or waiting on a worker via parallel-query wait) and worker waiting for the same extension lock (or vice versa) forms a deadlock invisible to deadlock.c because the group is treated as one entity; also group-held locks are conceptually shared so the lock can be released/held inconsistently (holder-in-group check, LockCheckConflicts). Result: undetected deadlock / hang. Actually the follow-up reverted to the original semantic: relation extension and page locks must not conflict in group, as they are held only briefly and not deadlock-detectable. Violates deadlock-freedom (detectability).
CONFIDENCE: medium for (a); low for (b)
DRILL: Read LockCheckConflicts in lock.c and the comments about group locking for relation extension and page locks (lock.c / README).
