## R1-04
FILES: commands/async.c
ANSWER: pg_listening_channels walks the shared dshash table (global channel table) with a dshash_seq scan kept open across calls of the SRF. A dshash sequential scan holds partition locks (LWLocks) until it ends. Holding them between calls means: a long or abandoned query (error, cancel, LIMIT, not fetched to the end) leaks the lock or fails to terminate the scan; other sessions' LISTEN/UNLISTEN and NOTIFY block; lock-order or self-deadlock is possible. This breaks the rule that LWLocks must be short-term and released on every exit path. The fix is to collect the channel names into local memory in the first call (scan opened and closed inside one call) and return them one per call.
CONFIDENCE: medium for (a); medium for (b)
DRILL: Read pg_listening_channels in commands/async.c and the dshash_seq_init/term calls and the lock's lifetime.

## R1-06
FILES: utils/cache/plancache.c, utils/cache/inval.c, utils/cache/syscache.c, utils/misc/rls.c, rewrite/rowsecurity.c
ANSWER: Register syscache invalidation callbacks in the plan cache for the role catalogs (pg_authid, pg_auth_members) and the database owner (pg_database). When one fires, invalidate every CachedPlanSource marked as RLS-dependent (dependsOnRLS / role-dependent). The plans are replanned lazily at next use. The invalidation is coarse (all RLS-dependent plans), not tracked per relation, because plancache only tracks relation/function/type dependencies precisely (D-plancache-lazy-inval) and the role-dependence of a policy cannot be tied to a particular relation. Plans that do not depend on RLS are left alone.

DRILL: Read plancache.c (PlanCacheRelCallback, PlanCacheSysCallback, the dependsOnRLS handling) and the syscache callback registration in InitPlanCache.
