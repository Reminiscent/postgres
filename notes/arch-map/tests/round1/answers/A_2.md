## B2-06
FILES: src/backend/optimizer/path/joinpath.c, src/backend/executor/nodeMemoize.c, src/backend/optimizer/plan/createplan.c
ANSWER: Memoize used the type's default (btree/hash opclass) equality to compare cache keys, but the join clause's operator may be coarser-equal-than-default... actually finer: the cache must distinguish exactly what the join operator distinguishes. Fix: use the join clause's own equality operator (and its hash function/collation) for the cache-key hashing and comparison, i.e. record the actual operator from the clause in the Memoize path/plan (hash operators list) and look up its hash function, instead of the type's default opclass. Constraint: cache key equality must be no coarser than the join operator's, else different outer values share entries.
CONFIDENCE: medium for (a); medium for (b)
DRILL: Read paraminfo_get_equal_hashops in joinpath.c and ExecInitMemoize in nodeMemoize.c.

## B2-11
FILES: src/backend/commands/trigger.c, src/backend/utils/adt/ri_triggers.c, src/backend/access/transam/xact.c
ANSWER: Resetting the counter to zero at subtransaction end would be wrong because a subtransaction can be nested inside an active firing loop in the outer (sub)transaction, where the depth is legitimately nonzero; zeroing and clearing the callbacks would break the enclosing loop.
